Common questions

Where is my data stored?
On your device only. Clockr has no account and no cloud sync, so nothing is uploaded. See the privacy policy.
I forgot to check out. Can I fix it?
Yes. A session with no check-out is flagged as missing. You can add the check-out from the Timeline tab and the session will be counted normally.
How do I change my daily target?
Open Settings, then adjust the hours for each weekday. Set Saturday and Sunday to zero if you don't work weekends — the app will stop counting them.
Does the widget update?
The Home Screen widget and Live Activity refresh when you check in or out. If a widget looks stale, open the app once and it will resync.
How do I delete my data?
Delete the app. Everything it stored goes with it.
