Clockr

Privacy Policy

Clockr does not collect your data. Everything you record stays on your device.

What Clockr stores

Clockr records the times you check in and check out, along with the work schedule you configure (daily targets and weekend days). This information is written to your device's local storage.

Where it goes

Nowhere. Clockr has no account system, no servers and no cloud sync. Your times are never transmitted off your device.

The app and its Home Screen widget share your times through an Apple App Group container, which is a private storage area on your device. This sharing never leaves the device.

What Clockr does not do

  • No analytics or telemetry
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No third-party SDKs
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No access to contacts, photos, location, health data or the microphone

Deleting your data

Your data belongs to you. Deleting Clockr from your device removes everything it stored. There is no copy anywhere else to request or delete.

Children

Clockr collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the revised version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
